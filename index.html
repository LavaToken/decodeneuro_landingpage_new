How Decode Neuro guides
a recovering brain.

A non-invasive headset reads brain and movement signals during therapy — no implant, and no interruption to the session.

Neural models turn those signals into markers of motor intent, effort, fatigue, and what the brain is relearning.

Therapists see what is responding in the moment and adjust the next repetition, the dose, and the plan for going home.
